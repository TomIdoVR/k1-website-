# KabatOne — Off-Site Authority Target List

> Created 2026-07-06. The on-site SEO is mature; the **ceiling** for the money hubs
> (VMS pos ~15, video-analytics pos ~18) breaking onto page 1 is **domain authority**.
> This is an off-site campaign — it needs accounts, outreach, and human execution
> (Claude Code can't create listings or do outreach). Prioritized by impact × effort.

## Why this matters now
Non-branded organic clicks went 10× (4→39) and pages are climbing — but the hubs
are stuck on page 2 because competitors (Genetec, Milestone, Motorola, Verkada) have
years of backlinks. On-page relevance is done; **links are the remaining lever.**

## Tier 1 — B2B software directories (highest ROI, do first)
These give trust signals, referral traffic from in-market buyers, and citations AI
engines read. Most are free to list; "claim + complete profile" is the task.

|---|---|---|---|
| **G2.com** | #1 B2B review site; ranks for "best X software"; AI engines cite it | Marketing | M — claim, complete, seed 5–10 reviews from customers |
| **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** (Gartner-owned network) | One submission → 3 directories; huge "software" query coverage | Marketing | M |
| **Gartner Peer Insights** | Enterprise/gov buyer trust; category listing | Marketing | M |
| **TrustRadius** | Detailed reviews, good for gov procurement | Marketing | S |
| **SourceForge / Slashdot** | Free listing, dofollow, indexes fast | Marketing | S |

**Target categories:** Video Management Software (VMS), Video Analytics, Computer-Aided
Dispatch (CAD), Public Safety, Physical Security — one listing per category = coverage
of the exact clusters we're optimizing on-site.
