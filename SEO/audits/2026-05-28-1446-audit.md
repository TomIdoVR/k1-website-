**28-day window: 11,564 impressions · 84 clicks · 0.73% CTR · 723 unique queries · 70 unique pages with impressions.**

CTR is well below the typical 2–3% benchmark — the site is being *shown* but not *clicked*. Root cause: 95% of clicks come from branded queries ("kabat one" / "kabatone"), while non-branded queries — where the impression volume lives — are converting near zero. Most non-branded impressions are at positions 15–60 (page 2+), where CTR collapses naturally.
