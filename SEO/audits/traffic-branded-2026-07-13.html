Recommendation — fight for longtail

1. Prioritise the 198-query striking-distance stack over the 5 head terms. ~2× the click prize, and these sit at pos 6–15 (winnable) vs 17+ on head terms dominated by incumbents.
2. Most are at pos ~8 with 0% CTR — a snippet/CTR problem, not authority. They're already on page 1. Title + meta + FAQ-schema tuning to beat the AI Overview (the v2.270 C5-ES fix) is the cheap, fast lever. Replicate it across the CAD and Video longtail.
3. Build spokes off the three cluster hubs — CAD (best-cad-dispatch-software), C5, VMS — targeting exact longtail phrasings. This is the proven play (VMS recovered 34→10 via spokes).
4. Brand needs no SEO work — kabatone / kabat one already rank #1 at 47% CTR. Brand demand is flat, so every incremental click has to come from non-branded longtail.
